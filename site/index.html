Hello, world.

A canvas for apps that can change.

An experiment in software you can reshape—with a prompt, a version history, and a way back.

Named in honour of Kurt Friedrich Gödel.

Gödel explored the limits of formal proof. Here, the question is what happens when software can inspect and rewrite its own files, while keeping earlier versions within reach.
